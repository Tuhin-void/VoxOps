MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES

==================================================
PUMP P101 — BEARING REPLACEMENT
==================================================
Frequency: every 4000 operating hours or on vibration alarm.
Tools: bearing puller, torque wrench (range 20-100 Nm), oil pan.

Steps:
1. Lock out / tag out the pump motor breaker.
2. Drain the lubrication oil into the pan.
3. Remove the coupling guard and disconnect the coupling.
4. Remove the bearing housing bolts (8 x M12 at 60 Nm).
5. Pull out the rotor assembly using the bearing puller.
6. Replace SKF 6310 bearings on both drive and non-drive ends.
7. Refill with ISO VG 68 oil to the sight glass mid-mark.
8. Recommission and verify vibration is below 4.5 mm/s RMS.

Last serviced (P101): 2026-04-12 — bearings replaced, oil changed,
seal inspected (OK). Vibration after service: 1.9 mm/s RMS.

==================================================
VALVE V203 — SEAT INSERT REPLACEMENT
==================================================
Frequency: on failure to reseat, or every 24 months.
Tools: torque wrench, valve seat extractor, lapping compound.

Steps:
1. Isolate the line and depressurize.
2. Remove the bonnet bolts (6 x M16 at 130 Nm).
3. Lift the bonnet and pilot assembly clear.
4. Extract the worn seat insert; clean the seat pocket.
5. Install seat insert kit V203-SI and lap to 0.8 micron finish.
6. Reassemble with a new bonnet gasket V203-BG-001.
7. Hydrotest at 15.0 bar, hold for 10 minutes.
8. Verify set pressure 8.0 bar +/- 0.2 bar on a test rig.

Last serviced (V203): 2026-02-03 — seat insert replaced, hydrotest passed.

==================================================
FILTER F22 — MEDIA REPLACEMENT
==================================================
Frequency: when differential pressure reaches 250 Pa, or every 6 months.

Steps:
1. Stop the air handling unit.
2. Open the filter access panel.
3. Slide out the spent filter cartridge F22-MC-13.
4. Wipe the frame; verify gasket integrity.
5. Insert the new cartridge with the airflow arrow oriented correctly.
6. Close the panel and verify pressure drop is below 100 Pa at full flow.

Last serviced (F22): 2026-05-20 — media replaced, gasket inspected (OK).

==================================================
GENERAL SAFETY
==================================================
- Always perform lock out / tag out on rotating equipment.
- Wear appropriate PPE: safety glasses, gloves, hearing protection.
- Two-person rule when working on energized systems.
- Document every action in the work order before closing it.
