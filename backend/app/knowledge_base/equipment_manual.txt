EQUIPMENT MANUAL — PUMPS, VALVES, AND FILTERS

==================================================
PUMP P101 — CENTRIFUGAL PUMP
==================================================
Model: AcmeCentriflow 4500
Type: Centrifugal, single-stage
Rated flow: 250 m3/h
Rated head: 45 m
Inlet/Outlet: DN150 / DN125 flanged
Motor: 75 kW, 3-phase, 415 V
Bearing type: Deep groove ball bearing, SKF 6310
Lubrication: Mineral oil ISO VG 68, change every 4000 hours

Operating parameters:
- Maximum suction pressure: 3.5 bar
- Discharge pressure (normal): 6.2 bar
- Operating temperature: 5–80 C
- Vibration limit (steady state): 4.5 mm/s RMS

Common faults:
- Vibration above 4.5 mm/s typically indicates worn bearings or impeller
  imbalance. Replace bearings and check impeller balance.
- Cavitation noise indicates low suction head; verify upstream strainer.
- Leakage at shaft seal: replace mechanical seal kit MSK-150.

==================================================
VALVE V203 — PRESSURE CONTROL VALVE
==================================================
Model: FlowSafe PRV-203
Type: Pilot-operated pressure relief valve
Set pressure: 8.0 bar
Reseat pressure: 7.2 bar
Body material: Carbon steel ASTM A216 WCB
Trim material: Stainless steel 316
Connection: 2" 150# flanged inlet, 3" 150# flanged outlet

Operating pressure: rated up to 10.0 bar working, 15.0 bar test.
The valve must reseat fully within 0.5 bar of its set pressure.

Common faults:
- Chatter at set point: clean the pilot orifice and check for debris.
- Failure to reseat: replace the seat insert kit V203-SI.
- External leakage at bonnet: replace bonnet gasket V203-BG-001.

==================================================
FILTER F22 — AIR FILTER
==================================================
Model: AirPure F22
Type: Pleated synthetic media, MERV 13
Dimensions: 600 x 600 x 100 mm
Recommended differential pressure (clean): 80 Pa
Replacement threshold: 250 Pa or 6 months, whichever comes first.

Common faults:
- High pressure drop without dust loading: media is wet, replace immediately.
- Bypass leakage: re-seat the filter frame, check for warped gasket.

Spare parts catalogue:
- Bearing SKF 6310 (for P101)
- Mechanical seal kit MSK-150 (for P101)
- Pilot orifice cleaning kit V203-PCK
- Seat insert kit V203-SI
- Bonnet gasket V203-BG-001
- Filter media cartridge F22-MC-13
